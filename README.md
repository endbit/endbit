## About

I didn't come into software through the traditional path.

Building automations, integrations, and internal tools for businesses quickly exposed the same recurring problem: operational friction.

Disconnected systems, manual processes, lost opportunities, scattered information, and decisions being made without context.

That naturally pushed me toward business software, workflow automation, AI-assisted systems, and operational platforms designed to connect processes instead of creating more complexity.

The more I work around AI, the less interested I become in flashy demos and the more interested I become in systems that quietly improve how businesses operate every day.

Most of what I build today revolves around automation, orchestration, operational visibility, and software that helps businesses make better decisions with less effort.

---

## Lead Recall AI

Lead Recall AI started from a simple observation.

Many businesses lose potential customers not because they lack leads, but because follow-ups are forgotten, inventory changes go unnoticed, and operational processes break down over time.

Lead Recall AI was designed to solve that problem.

The platform monitors conversations, detects purchase intent using AI, connects customers with available inventory, and generates new commercial opportunities automatically.

What began as a lead recovery system evolved into a broader exploration of event-driven architectures, operational intelligence, AI workflows, and business automation.
