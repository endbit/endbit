## Featured Project

# Lead Recall AI

AI-powered lead recovery platform designed to help businesses recover lost sales opportunities automatically.

Many companies lose potential customers because conversations are forgotten, inventory changes go unnoticed, or follow-ups never happen.

### What it does

- Monitors customer conversations
- Detects purchase intent using AI
- Matches customers with available inventory
- Generates new sales opportunities automatically
- Supports commercial teams with actionable insights

- Connect information

- Support better decisions

Technology should quietly help businesses operate better.
